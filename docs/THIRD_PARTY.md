- **Wallet marks**: the MetaMask, Coinbase, Rabby and Phantom SVGs were extracted from `@rainbow-me/rainbowkit@2.2.11`, preserving vendor colors and source artwork. Their package MIT notice ships at `web/public/licenses/RainbowKit-MIT.txt` and `dist/licenses/RainbowKit-MIT.txt`. No RainbowKit runtime or dependency archive is included.
- **Fonts**: Manrope and JetBrains Mono variable Latin WOFF2 files are self-hosted from the locked Fontsource packages. The original OFL text is retained in `web/public/licenses/` and exported to `dist/licenses/`.
- **Runtime packages**: React, viem, wagmi, TanStack Query and Coinbase Wallet SDK, with their installed production dependencies. Versions are locked in `web/package-lock.json`. `web/scripts/licenses.mjs` produces `web/public/licenses/ThirdPartyNotices.txt`; it also ships in the export. The required Apache-2.0 full license is included separately.
- **REKTD visual assets**: the R mark/favicon and static share card are original code-drawn SVG artwork for this frontend. The share-card source is `web/artwork/share.svg`; `web/scripts/share.mjs` renders its committed PNG with Playwright. No external image or font service is used at runtime.

Implementation references consulted alongside the pinned inputs:

- [Uniswap v4 swap sequence](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/v4/guides/swapping/swapping) and [Universal Router commands](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/protocols/universal-router/concepts/commands). Robinhood's extended swap tuple is controlled by the supplied network table, not inferred from the generic guide.
- [wagmi wallet integration](https://wagmi.sh/react/guides/connect-wallet), plus the installed, locked connector declarations. The implementation uses the specific `injected` and `coinbaseWallet` entrypoints, without pulling in relay connectors.
- [Rabby mobile's universal-link handler](https://github.com/RabbyHub/rabby-mobile/blob/develop/apps/mobile/src/hooks/universalLink.ts) establishes `_cmd=open-dapp&dapp=<encoded page URL>` at `https://go.rabby.io/mobile/`. MetaMask, Coinbase and Phantom links follow the pinned wallet connector guide. Phone links were checked for the exact current-page payload; opening a physical app was not tested.
